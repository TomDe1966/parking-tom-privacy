📋 How to Request Account Deletion

To delete your account and all associated data from Parking Tom, please follow these steps:

Step 1: Send an email to our support team at tomspotifaj@gmail.com
Step 2: Include the following information in your email:
  • Subject line: "Account Deletion Request"
  • Your registered email address
  • Confirmation that you want to delete your account
Step 3: Wait for confirmation from our team (processing time: up to 30 days)
📧 Send Deletion Request Email

🗂️ What Data Will Be Deleted?

Upon account deletion, the following data will be permanently removed:

  • Account Information: Email address and encrypted password
  • Saved Locations: All GPS coordinates and location names you've saved
  • Timestamps: All dates and times associated with your saved locations
  • Custom Labels: Item names and selected icons
  • User Preferences: Any app settings or configurations

⏱️ Data Retention Period

Once your deletion request is processed:

  • Immediate deletion: Your account will be deactivated immediately
  • Complete removal: All associated data will be permanently deleted from our Firebase database within 30 days
  • Backup systems: Data in backup systems will be deleted within 90 days maximum
⚠️ Warning: Account deletion is permanent and irreversible. You will lose access to:
  • All saved parking locations
  • Your account credentials
  • Any shared locations with family members

Please make sure you have backed up any important location data before requesting deletion.
